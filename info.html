Our Story

What is Sudarshan Chakra India?

Sudarshan Chakra India is a hyperlocal service marketplace built for the heart of India — its skilled, hardworking people. Named after the sacred spinning disc of Lord Vishnu, our platform embodies perpetual motion, protection, and prosperity for every community it serves.

We bridge the gap between service seekers — families and individuals who need reliable, trusted help at home — and service providers — the plumbers, electricians, teachers, beauticians, cooks, and countless other skilled workers who form the backbone of India's economy.

Unlike global platforms, Sudarshan Chakra India is built ground-up for India: supporting 12 regional languages, respecting India's diverse religions and communities, working on low-bandwidth connections, and prioritising dignity and fair earnings for every worker on the platform.

Our platform is more than a marketplace — it is a movement to formalise the informal economy, build trust through verified profiles and genuine reviews, and give every skilled Indian the digital presence they deserve.

User Manual

How to Use the App

Getting started is simple. Follow these steps to get the most out of Sudarshan Chakra India.

👤 For Service Seekers

1

Choose Your Language

Tap 🌏 in the top navigation bar and select your preferred language. Every screen, label and category instantly switches to your language.

2

Browse or Voice-Search

Use the search bar, tap 🎤 to speak your need, or tap Browse Services to explore 50+ categories like Plumbing, Electrical, Beauty, Tutoring, AC Repair and more.

3

View Provider Profiles

Tap any provider card to see their full profile — photo, services, hourly rate, working hours, location, portfolio, reviews and verification status.

4

Call, WhatsApp or Hire

Contact the provider directly via 📞 Call or 💬 WhatsApp, or tap Hire Now to raise a formal booking request with date, location and details.

5

Login & Earn Rewards

Login with your mobile number (OTP — no password needed). Save favourite providers, leave reviews, refer friends and earn Wallet points with every action.

⚙️ For Service Providers

1

Register Your Profile

Tap Register as Provider. Fill in your name, mobile, category, service area, working hours and hourly rate. Upload a clear, professional profile photo.

2

Verify Your Identity

Upload your Aadhaar, Driving Licence or Voter ID (optional but highly recommended). Verified providers earn a ✅ badge and appear higher in search results.

3

Add Portfolio Photos

After logging in, visit your profile and upload photos of your past work. A strong portfolio of 5–10 photos significantly increases booking conversion.

4

Upgrade Your Membership

Choose Basic (free), Professional or Elite membership for greater visibility, priority listing, and more features. Use earned Wallet points to reduce the upgrade cost.

5

Refer & Earn

Share your unique referral link from your Wallet page. Earn points every time a new provider joins through your link. Build a team — earn more together.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often from our community.

Browsing and searching for providers is completely free for seekers. Providers can register and list their basic profile at no cost. Premium membership tiers (Professional and Elite) are available for providers who want greater visibility and features, with pricing that can be partially offset using earned Wallet points.
We use secure mobile OTP login — no password needed. Enter your 10-digit mobile number, receive a 6-digit SMS code, enter it and you're in. Your session persists across visits so you won't need to login every time on the same device.
Yes. Your mobile number is used only for OTP verification and is never displayed publicly. Identity verification documents are seen exclusively by our admin team and are never shared publicly. We do not sell your data to any third party under any circumstances.
You earn points for daily logins (2 pts), leaving reviews (5 pts), completing bookings, and referring new users. Points accumulate in your Wallet and can be redeemed to offset membership upgrade fees. A portion can also be donated to our Charity program to fund skill development for underprivileged workers.
The Verified badge is awarded to providers who upload a valid government ID (Aadhaar, Driving Licence, Voter ID, PAN or Passport) for admin review. Once approved, the badge appears prominently on your profile, significantly increasing seeker trust and booking rates.
Absolutely. Sudarshan Chakra India fully supports 12 languages: English, Hindi, Bengali, Gujarati, Marathi, Kannada, Telugu, Malayalam, Tamil, Punjabi, Odia and Assamese. Tap 🌏 in the navigation bar at any time to switch languages. The entire app — every label, category and form — instantly updates to your chosen language.
On any provider's profile, tap the 🚩 Report button, describe your concern and submit. Our admin team reviews all reports within 24 hours. Providers with repeated or serious reports are investigated and may be suspended or removed from the platform.
Our Charity program lets you donate your earned Wallet points to a fund that sponsors vocational skill training for underprivileged youth. Even a small donation contributes to a training programme that can transform someone's livelihood. You can donate via the Wallet section of your profile.
Every registered user gets a unique referral link accessible from their Wallet page. Share it with friends and colleagues. When they register as a provider through your link, you earn Wallet points. You can track all referrals, earnings and referral history in your Wallet dashboard.
Leave an honest review with your star rating — this protects the next seeker. Use the Report feature for serious issues. For booking disputes, contact us via the Help section below and our team will mediate. Building a transparent, accountable community is at the core of what we do.
Sudarshan Chakra India is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On Android: open the site in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu and select "Add to Home Screen". On iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". The app icon will appear on your home screen just like a native app, and it works offline too.
