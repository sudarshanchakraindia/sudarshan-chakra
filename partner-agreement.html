📜 Agreement & Terms and Conditions

1. Appointment

Sudarshan Chakra India ("the Company") appoints the applicant as a non-exclusive City Channel Partner ("Partner") for the city and pincode(s) specified in this application, subject to the terms below.

2. Role & Responsibilities

  • Onboard genuine service providers and seekers onto the SAARTHI platform within the assigned territory.
  • Represent the Company honestly and professionally at all times.
  • Not make promises, guarantees, or commitments on behalf of the Company beyond what is officially communicated.
  • Not collect any payment directly from providers or seekers on behalf of the Company.

3. Revenue Share

The Partner shall earn a percentage share of the seeker convenience fee and provider membership upgrades attributable to their referral code or assigned pincode, as communicated separately by the Company from time to time. Payouts are subject to verification and are made periodically at the Company's discretion.

4. TDS & Taxation

Payouts to the Partner may be subject to Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) as per applicable Indian tax law. The Partner's PAN is collected for this purpose. The Partner is responsible for their own tax filings and compliance.

5. Term & Termination

This arrangement may be terminated by either party at any time, with or without cause, by written notice (including email or WhatsApp). Upon termination, the Partner's referral code and pincode attribution will be deactivated, and any pending verified payouts will be settled.

6. Confidentiality

The Partner agrees to keep confidential any non-public business information, pricing, or user data shared by the Company, and not to misuse or share such information with third parties.

7. Independent Relationship

The Partner is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, or legal representative of the Company. Nothing in this agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship.

8. Data & Documents

Identity documents (Aadhaar, PAN, photo) are collected solely for verification and TDS compliance purposes and will be handled in accordance with the Company's data protection practices.

9. Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising from this agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which they shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts at Jodhpur, Rajasthan.

This is a standard template agreement. The Company reserves the right to update these terms; material changes will be communicated to active Partners.
