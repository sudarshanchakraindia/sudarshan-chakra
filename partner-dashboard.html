🚀 Getting Started — How This Works

Share your referral link

Send your link (above) via WhatsApp, or show the QR code in person. It takes people straight into the Sudarshan Chakra India app.

Help providers register

Guide local plumbers, electricians, beauticians, tutors etc. through the "Register as Provider" flow — they pick a category, add their details, and submit an ID for verification.

Get providers ID-verified

Verified providers earn more trust from seekers and are more likely to get hired — encourage everyone you onboard to complete ID verification.

Promote to seekers too

The more seekers actively using the app in your city, the more your onboarded providers get hired — and the more you earn.

📖 Partner FAQ

How do I earn money? +
You earn 25% of the ₹51 platform convenience fee on every completed hire involving a provider or seeker you onboarded, plus 20% of membership plan revenue (₹151 Silver / ₹251 Gold) when your onboarded providers upgrade. Payouts are logged below and paid monthly via UPI.
What counts as "my" provider or seeker? +
Anyone who registers using your referral link or QR code is automatically attributed to you — permanently, as long as they stay active on the platform.
What if someone registers without my link? +
Even if the person registers without using your specific referral link or QR code, they'll still be credited to you as long as the pincode they enter matches the pincode assigned to your city. Your link/QR is still the more reliable way to guarantee credit, but pincode matching covers people who find the app on their own.
What is the activity requirement? +
To keep your city exclusivity, you need to onboard at least 33 verified providers and 66 seekers within your first 30 days, and maintain similar monthly activity after that. This target applies for your first year as a partner — Sudarshan Chakra India already lists 525+ services with room to keep growing, so this is set to match real market scale, not an arbitrary minimum. It may be revised in future years as the platform and each city's market mature. This dashboard's progress bar tracks it live.
Where do I get marketing materials? +
See the "Share & Promote" section above — it generates a poster personalized with your own name, phone number, and your tracked QR code automatically. Use "Share My Poster" to send it directly to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or any app on your phone, or "Download My Poster" to save it first. Generic (non-personalized) materials are also available further down if you prefer those.
Does my shared QR/link still track location? +
Yes — every link and QR code generated for you (including the one baked into your personalized poster) includes location tracking automatically. This isn't for surveillance; it's what lets the central team confirm scans are genuinely coming from your assigned city, which protects your exclusivity and credibility as a partner.
