SUDARSHAN CHAKRA INDIA (SAARTHI)

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SUDARSHAN CHAKRA INDIA ("we", "us", "SAARTHI") collects, uses and protects your information when you use our website and web app at sudarshanchakraindia.github.io/sudarshan-chakra, our City Channel Partner pages, and our WhatsApp Business messaging.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, mobile number (for OTP login), email (for partners and admins), city, pincode and preferred language.
  • Service provider profile: services offered, rates, experience, availability, languages, photos and other details you choose to add.
  • Location: your device location, only when you allow it, to show nearby providers and distance.
  • Bookings, payments and wallet: hires, reviews, membership plan, referral codes and reward points. UPI payments are completed in your own UPI app; we do not collect or store your UPI PIN or bank account/card details.
  • City Channel Partner applications: details on the application form and identity documents (such as Aadhaar or PAN) uploaded for verification, plus your digital consent to the partner agreement.
  • WhatsApp messages: your WhatsApp number, your opt-in/consent record, and the messages you send to or receive from our WhatsApp Business number, including delivery status.
  • Voice features: voice search and the RADHEY assistant use your browser's speech features; we save only the details you confirm (for example during voice registration).
  • Technical data: basic device, browser and usage information needed to run and secure the app.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account, log you in and verify your identity.
  • To connect service seekers with suitable nearby providers.
  • To process bookings, platform fees, memberships, referrals and rewards.
  • To review and manage City Channel Partner applications and agreements.
  • To send WhatsApp messages you have agreed to receive, such as invitations, service updates and replies to your queries.
  • To provide support, prevent fraud and misuse, and keep the platform secure.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. WhatsApp messaging and opt-in

We send WhatsApp template messages only to numbers that have given consent (opt-in). You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP on WhatsApp or by emailing us. Your use of WhatsApp is also covered by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

4. Sharing of information

  • Between users: relevant profile and contact details are shared between a seeker and a provider when a connection or booking is made.
  • Service providers we use: Google Firebase (database, login, file storage and cloud functions), Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), GitHub Pages (website hosting) and OpenStreetMap/Leaflet (maps). They process data on our behalf to run the service.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data storage and security

Data is stored on Google Firebase servers. Access is restricted using authentication and security rules, and identity documents are visible only to authorised administrators. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active or as long as needed for the purposes above, and as required by law. Partner verification documents are kept for the duration of the partnership and as legally required.

7. Your rights and data deletion

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, at any time.

How to request deletion: email sudarshanchakraindia@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and your registered mobile number. We will confirm and delete your account data, including WhatsApp message records, within 30 days, except where we must keep some records by law.

8. Children

Our services are meant for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated date.
