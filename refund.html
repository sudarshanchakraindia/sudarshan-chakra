This policy explains when payments made on SUDARSHAN CHAKRA INDIA (SAARTHI) can be refunded. Please read it together with our Terms & Conditions.

1. Portal fee (₹51) paid by Seekers

The ₹51 portal fee pays for unlocking a Provider's Call and WhatsApp contact for 48 hours. Once the contact has been unlocked the service is delivered, so the fee is normally not refundable. You will get a full refund in these cases:

  • Money deducted but contact not unlocked because of a technical failure.
  • Duplicate payment — you were charged more than once for the same Provider in the same 48-hour period.
  • Provider not reachable: the Provider's number is wrong, switched off or not in service, and you report it to us within 48 hours of payment. We will check this before approving the refund.
  • Provider listing removed by us (for example, for fraud) within 48 hours of your payment.

A refund is not given only because the Seeker and Provider could not agree on price, timing or the work, or because the Seeker changed their mind after the contact was unlocked.

2. Provider membership plans

  • You can cancel a paid plan (Professional / Gold) within 7 days of payment for a full refund, if you have not received any hire, lead or paid benefit from the plan in that time.
  • After 7 days, or once plan benefits have been used, the fee for the current period is not refundable. You can stop renewal at any time.
  • Duplicate payments, or payments deducted without the plan being activated, are always fully refunded.

3. Tips and charity

  • Tips are passed on to the Provider and are not refundable, except when charged twice by mistake or deducted without being recorded.
  • Charity donations are not refundable once made, except for duplicate or failed transactions.

4. Wallet points

Reward points have no cash value and cannot be refunded or converted to money.

5. How to ask for a refund

  • Email sudarshanchakraindia@gmail.com or call +91 79767 85013 within 7 days of the payment.
  • Share your registered mobile number, the payment/transaction ID, the date and amount, and the reason.
  • We will reply within 2 working days.

6. Refund timeline

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method (UPI / card / bank account) within 5–7 working days. After that, the time for the money to show in your account depends on your bank or UPI app.

7. Cancellation of service work

The Portal does not collect payment for the Provider's work. Any advance or payment for the work itself is agreed between the Seeker and the Provider, and its cancellation or refund must be settled between them. We will help by sharing your complaint with the Provider and, where needed, taking action on the Provider's listing.

1. सीकर द्वारा दी गई पोर्टल फीस (₹51)

₹51 की पोर्टल फीस से किसी प्रोवाइडर का Call और WhatsApp संपर्क 48 घंटे के लिए खुलता है। संपर्क खुलते ही सेवा पूरी मानी जाती है, इसलिए यह फीस सामान्यतः वापस नहीं होती। इन स्थितियों में पूरा रिफंड मिलेगा:

  • पैसा कट गया पर संपर्क नहीं खुला, किसी तकनीकी ख़राबी की वजह से।
  • दोहरा भुगतान: एक ही प्रोवाइडर के लिए 48 घंटे के अंदर एक से ज़्यादा बार पैसा कटा।
  • प्रोवाइडर से संपर्क नहीं हो सका: उसका नंबर गलत, बंद या सेवा में नहीं है, और आपने भुगतान के 48 घंटे के अंदर हमें बताया। रिफंड से पहले हम इसकी जाँच करेंगे।
  • आपके भुगतान के 48 घंटे के अंदर हमने प्रोवाइडर की लिस्टिंग हटा दी हो (जैसे धोखाधड़ी के कारण)।

सिर्फ़ इस कारण रिफंड नहीं मिलेगा कि सीकर और प्रोवाइडर के बीच कीमत, समय या काम पर बात नहीं बनी, या संपर्क खुलने के बाद सीकर ने अपना मन बदल लिया।
