# 🌟 Sudarshan Chakra India — SAARTHI

**Bharat Ka Apna Service App — India's Hyperlocal Service Marketplace**

---

## 🎯 About

Sudarshan Chakra India (product name: **SAARTHI**) connects seekers with verified local service providers — plumbers, electricians, beauticians, tutors and hundreds more — with zero broker commission. Providers keep 100% of what they earn; the platform runs on a small convenience fee from seekers and optional provider memberships.

- 🗣️ **Language** — 12 Indian languages, fully localized
- 📍 **Proximity** — Map view and distance-based matching
- ⭐ **Trust** — ID-verified providers, genuine reviews only
- 🎤 **Voice-first** — RADHEY, the built-in voice assistant
- 💰 **Fair economics** — No commission on provider earnings

---

## ✨ Features

### For Seekers
- 🎤 **RADHEY Voice Assistant** — search, register or get help hands-free, in your language
- 🔍 **Smart Search & Filters** — by category, distance, rating, language, price
- 🗺️ **Interactive Map** — see providers near you
- ✅ **Verified Badges** — Aadhaar/PAN/Voter ID-verified providers
- 💬 **In-App Chat, Call & WhatsApp** — no need to share numbers publicly
- 📅 **Easy Booking** — schedule with date, location and job details
- ⭐ **Genuine Reviews** — only verified interactions can leave a review
- 💳 **UPI Payments** — QR-based, PhonePe/GPay/Paytm compatible
- 🎁 **Wallet & Referral Points** — earn on logins, reviews, referrals; donate to the Charity program
- 🎥 **Free Training Videos** — curated content for using the app and growing skills
- 📲 **Installable PWA** — add to home screen, works like a native app, no app store needed
- 100% free to search, browse and contact providers

### For Providers
- 📝 **Quick Registration** — with RADHEY voice-guided signup
- 📸 **Portfolio Showcase** — up to 10 work photos
- 🕐 **Flexible Availability** — Mon-Fri, weekends, all days, or 24×7
- 📍 **Service Area** — fixed radius or city-wide
- 🏅 **Membership Plans**:
  - **Basic** — Free, standard listing
  - **Silver** — ₹151/quarter — better visibility, lower fees
  - **Gold** — ₹251/quarter — priority placement, maximum visibility
- 💰 **Zero Commission** — keep 100% of earnings; only a small seeker-side convenience fee (₹51) supports the platform
- 🎥 **Free Skill Training** — grow your business with curated video lessons

### City Channel Partner Program
A city-level referral and revenue-share program for local business partners:
- 📍 **Pincode-based territory** — a unique referral code and auto-attribution by pincode
- 💰 **Revenue share** — a percentage of every convenience fee and membership upgrade from your referrals
- 🖼️ **Personalized marketing poster** — auto-generated with your name, phone and QR code, ready to print or share
- 📊 **Live Partner Dashboard** — track referred providers, seekers and earnings in real time
- 🎥 **Partner training videos** and a dedicated FAQ
- Managed via `partner-admin.html` (admin) and `partner-dashboard.html` (partner self-serve portal)

### Admin Tools
- Provider/category/subcategory/service management
- `training-admin.html` — manage Loom training videos by audience (Provider / Seeker / Partner)
- `partner-admin.html` — manage City Channel Partners and their payouts
- `qr-scans-viewer.html` — track QR code scan locations and sources
- Firebase-backed real-time data, with admin-gated write rules

## 📖 How to Use

### As a Seeker
1. Select your language (or let RADHEY guide you by voice)
2. Search or browse categories — 500+ services across 17 categories
3. Filter by distance, rating, language or price
4. View verified provider profiles and portfolios
5. Chat, call, or book directly — pay securely via UPI

### As a Provider
1. Click "Register Provider" (or use RADHEY's voice registration)
2. Add your details, service area, working hours and portfolio photos
3. Choose a plan — Basic (free), Silver, or Gold
4. Start receiving bookings and reviews

### As a City Channel Partner
1. Apply through the partner dashboard
2. Get your referral code, pincode territory and personalized poster
3. Onboard local providers and seekers
4. Track referrals and earnings live on your dashboard

## 🙏 About the Name

**Sudarshan Chakra** (सुदर्शन चक्र) — The divine discus that brings clarity and removes obstacles. Our platform aims to bring clarity to service seekers and remove obstacles in connecting with the right providers. **SAARTHI** (सारथी) — meaning "charioteer" or "guide" — is the product's public-facing identity: your guide to finding trusted help, and a guide for skilled workers to find steady income.
